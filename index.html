Dashboard Legend & Guide

Status & Stay Badges
5 Days18 Days35 Days
Time at CACC / Stay Duration: Shows how many days the dog has been at the shelter. Mapped to filters: Green (0-14 days; matches Fresh In for under 7 days), Yellow (15-29 days), and Red (30+ days; matches Long Stays). Tapping or clicking the badge reveals the exact date and time they were first listed.
Available
Available Status: Dogs currently at the shelter that are ready for adoption. Aligns with the Available filter.
Holds & Rescue Indicators
Hold for 3 days
Stray Hold: The mandatory holding period to give owners time to locate and reclaim their lost pet. The hold is 3 days for unchipped dogs and 7 days for chipped dogs.
Unavailable
Unavailable: The dog is newly arrived and undergoing intake processing, medical checks, or observation, and is not yet ready for adoption.
Rescue Only
Rescue Only: The dog can only be transferred to approved rescue organizations (not directly adopted by the public), usually due to special medical or behavioral needs.
Stray & Location Indicators
Stray
Stray Status: Found as a stray. Aligns with the Strays filter. If a location was recorded, you can click this tag to see where they were found on the map.
Stray
Chipped Stray: Found as a stray with a microchip. The dashed border simply highlights that a chip was detected (which gives them a 7-day holding period instead of 3 days).
C-70 C-70 C-70 C-70 C-70
Pavilion Location: Where the dog was last housed, read from the most recent handwritten walk sheet (pavilion letter + kennel number). A yellow or orange tint mirrors a marker highlight on the sheet — CACC's walker levels: yellow = intermediate-level dog, orange = advanced-level dog. Sheets are uploaded by onsite volunteers; each badge shows the pavilion's latest sheet and stays until a newer one replaces it. A dimmed badge means its sheet is more than a couple of days old — hover or tap for the sheet date. Spot a wrong one? Tap the small flag to report it — the badge stays visible with a warning mark (like the example with the triangle) until volunteers or an admin confirm the right kennel.
Special Profile Indicators
Return
Return Guest: This dog has been at CACC before. We detect this automatically by matching their intake ID against past shelter records. Aligns with the Returns filter.

Dog Name

FKA (Formerly Known As): Underlined names mean the dog has a known previous name. Tap or click the name on their card to switch between their current and past name.
Favorite
Favorites: Click the heart icon on any dog's card to save them. View your saved list anytime by clicking the Favorites filter.
Demographics & Attributes

Gender & Altered Status: Shows the dog's gender. A dashed border and scissors icon mean the dog is spayed or neutered. A solid border means CACC hasn't listed them as altered — not necessarily that they are unaltered. Aligns with the Male, Female, and S/N filters.
2 Years
Age: Approximate age. Aligns with the Puppies (under 12 months) and Seniors (5 years and older) filters.
Small
Small Dog Filter: Filters for breeds that typically weigh 25 lbs or less when fully grown. Aligns with the Small filter.
BlackTanBrindle
Color Pills: Show the primary colors of the dog's coat.
Outcome Badges & Today's Activity
Adopted: Date
Adopted: The dog has been adopted and has left the shelter! Aligns with the Adopted filter.
Returned: Date
Returned to Owner (RTO): Reunited with their family! Aligns with the RTO filter.
Rescue Partner
Rescued: Transferred to an approved rescue group. Tap or click to see the exact transfer date and time. Aligns with the Rescued filter.
Today
Today's Activity: A card highlighted with a gold border and a "Today" badge means their adoption or rescue happened today.
