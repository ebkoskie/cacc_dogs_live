Dashboard Legend & Guide

Status & Stay Badges
5 Days18 Days35 Days
Time at CACC / Stay Duration: Shows how many days the dog has been at the shelter. Mapped to filters: Green (0-14 days; matches Fresh In for under 7 days), Yellow (15-29 days), and Red (30+ days; matches Long Stays). Tapping or clicking the badge reveals the exact date and time they were first listed.
Available
Available Status: Dogs currently at the shelter that are ready for adoption. Aligns with the Available filter.
Holds & Rescue Indicators
Hold for 3 days · ends Nov 15
Stray Hold: The mandatory holding period to give owners time to locate and reclaim their lost pet. The hold is 3 days for unchipped dogs and 7 days for chipped dogs. The badge shows the days left and the date the hold ends. To list only dogs on hold, or only Rescue Only dogs, use Stray hold in Filters.
Unavailable
Unavailable: The dog is newly arrived and undergoing intake processing, medical checks, or observation, and is not yet ready for adoption.
Rescue Only
Rescue Only: The dog can only be transferred to approved rescue organizations (not directly adopted by the public), usually due to special medical or behavioral needs.
Stray & Location Indicators
Stray
Stray Status: Found as a stray. Aligns with the Strays filter. If a location was recorded, you can click this tag to see where they were found on the map.
Stray
Chipped Stray: Found as a stray with a microchip. The dashed border simply highlights that a chip was detected (which gives them a 7-day holding period instead of 3 days).
C-70 C-70 C-70 C-70 C-70
Pavilion Location: Where the dog was last housed, read from the most recent handwritten walk sheet (pavilion letter + kennel number). A yellow or orange tint mirrors a marker highlight on the sheet — CACC's walker levels: yellow = intermediate-level dog, orange = advanced-level dog. Sheets are uploaded by onsite volunteers; each badge shows the latest sheet with that kennel on it, and a photo of a partly filled sheet updates its own kennels while the others keep their earlier sheet for up to a week. A dimmed badge means its sheet is more than a couple of days old — hover or tap for the sheet date. Spot a wrong one? Tap the small flag to report it — the badge stays visible with a warning mark (like the example with the triangle) until volunteers or an admin confirm the right kennel. To list one pavilion or one walker level, use Filters.
Special Profile Indicators
Return
Return Guest: This dog has been at CACC before. We detect this automatically by matching their intake ID against past shelter records. Aligns with the Returns filter.

Dog Name

FKA (Formerly Known As): Underlined names mean the dog has a known previous name. Tap or click the name on their card to switch between their current and past name.
Handling Notes: A folded top corner means volunteers who walk this dog at the shelter logged how it handles: leash manners, energy, interests, commands and how it reacts to other dogs. Tap the folded corner, or double-tap the card, to read them. A dashed "＋" corner means no notes yet; volunteers can tap it to log a visit. To find dogs by the answers volunteers gave most often (energy, leash manners, interests, commands and more), use Volunteer notes in Filters.
Favorite
Favorites: Click the heart icon on any dog's card to save them. View your saved list anytime by clicking the Favorites filter.

Gender & Altered Status: Shows the dog's gender. A dashed border and scissors icon mean the dog is spayed or neutered. A solid border means CACC hasn't listed them as altered — not necessarily that they are unaltered. Aligns with the Male, Female, and S/N filters.

Walk Sheet Upload

Volunteering? Each pavilion posts a handwritten walk sheet of its dogs and kennels. One photo updates the kennel badge for every dog on that sheet.

  1. Find the walk sheet posted in the pavilion you're in (A to E).
  2. Open Walk Sheet Upload and tap Take photo of sheet: whole sheet, flat, well lit. No need to pick the pavilion; we read it from the sheet.
  3. Tick the Terms box (first time only) and tap Upload sheet. Badges usually update within a few minutes.
