Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this website (the "Site") collects and how it is used. The Site is an independent, non-commercial project maintained by a Chicago Animal Care & Control ("CACC") volunteer (the "Maintainer") and is not affiliated with CACC or the City of Chicago. By using the Site, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The Site does not require an account and does not ask general visitors for names, email addresses, or other contact details. We collect only the following:

  • Anonymous usage analytics. The Site uses PostHog (a third-party analytics service) in anonymous mode to understand how the Site is used. This captures aggregate, non-identifying information such as pages viewed, page-leave events, referring page, and general browser, device, and operating-system type, along with an approximate geographic region derived from your IP address. Analytics are configured not to build individual personal profiles. Analytics run only when enabled by the Maintainer.
  • Information stored on your device. The Site uses your browser's local storage to remember your light/dark theme preference. If you use the volunteer features, your browser may also save what you enter (such as a passcode) on your device for convenience. This information stays in your browser and is not transmitted to the Maintainer for tracking.
  • Volunteer uploads. If you are an authorized volunteer and use the Upload feature, the photo you submit, the pavilion you select, and your passcode are sent to a third-party serverless processing service to read kennel and name information. Photos are downscaled in your browser and image metadata (including EXIF location data) is stripped before upload.

2. How We Use Information

  • to operate, maintain, and improve the Site and understand which features are useful;
  • to remember your display preferences; and
  • to provide the volunteer upload feature and keep it secure against abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to serve advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

The Site relies on third-party providers that may process limited technical data (such as your IP address and request headers) in the course of delivering their service. These include our analytics provider (PostHog), content-delivery networks and font/script hosts, a bot-protection service used on the Upload page (Cloudflare Turnstile), the upload-processing service, and the third-party sites and mapping tiles the Site links to or embeds. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy, over which the Maintainer has no control.

4. Cookies and Do Not Track

The Site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Local storage is used as described above, and our analytics provider may set limited storage on your device to operate. You can clear or block local storage and cookies through your browser settings, though some features (such as remembering your theme) may stop working. Because there is no consistent industry standard, the Site does not respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

5. Your Choices

  • You can use a browser content blocker or privacy setting to block the analytics script.
  • You can clear your browser's stored site data at any time.
  • Because general analytics are anonymous and aggregated, the Maintainer generally cannot identify or single out an individual visitor's data.

6. Data Retention

Aggregate analytics are retained according to our analytics provider's retention settings. Uploaded walk-sheet information is used only to display location badges; each pavilion's sheet is retained until a newer sheet for that pavilion replaces it, and is deleted automatically after at most 30 days. Anonymous "wrong badge" reports tied to a sheet are cleared when that sheet is replaced and likewise auto-delete within 30 days. Volunteer correction votes and admin overrides remain short-lived (about one to two days). Information stored on your device persists until you clear it.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

The Maintainer may update this Policy by posting a revised version on this page with a new effective date. Your continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

9. Governing Law

This Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

10. Contact

For questions about this Policy or to make a privacy request, you may contact the Site's maintainer.
